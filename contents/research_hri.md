### Human-Robot Interaction for Mobility Assistance

Our research in **Human-Robot Interaction (HRI)** focuses on developing intuitive control systems for intelligent robotic walkers (*Smart Walkers*), designed to assist individuals with reduced mobility or cognitive impairments.

The primary goal is to transform a simple mechanical aid into a **collaborative partner** capable of understanding the user's intentions and reacting naturally.

#### Key Research Areas:

* **Intention Recognition**: We utilize force sensors and vision systems to interpret the user's push or desired direction, allowing the walker to provide proactive support without requiring excessive physical effort.
* **Shared Control Navigation**: We implement algorithms where the robot gently corrects the trajectory to avoid obstacles or falls, while still providing the user with a sense of total control over the movement.

Through clinical trials and real-world experiments, we aim to improve quality of life by ensuring **autonomy, safety, and dignity** in daily activities.
