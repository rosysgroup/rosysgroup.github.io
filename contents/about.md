The **Robotic Systems Group**, led by **Prof. Giovanni Muscato**, belongs to the [Department of Electrical, Electronic and Computer Engineering (DIEEI)](https://www.dieei.unict.it/) of the University of Catania. 
We boast long-standing experience in applied research for robotic systems, ranging from industrial manipulators to advanced aerial and land mobile platforms. Our core focus lies in the coordination of heterogeneous fleets and the development of intelligent autonomous navigation for robots in hostile environments.
